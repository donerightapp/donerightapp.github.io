Privacy policy

Done Right · updated November 25, 2026

Done Right is a quality-inspection app for insulation crews. It runs on Google Apps Script inside the Google account of the company that uses it.

What the app stores

Inspection records, photos, PDF reports and the list of users are saved in that company's own Google Sheets and Google Drive. Email reports are sent from that company's Google account. Done Right does not keep a separate copy of the data on its own servers.

Google sign-in and API access

The app uses your Google account only to confirm who you are and whether you are on the company's Users list.

The developer's deploy tool asks for two Google permissions, Apps Script projects and Apps Script deployments. It uses them only to upload new versions of the app's code to the developer's own Apps Script project. It does not read your email, files or contacts.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers. It is visible only to people the company has given access to, and to the developer when supporting the app.

Deleting data

The company that uses Done Right owns its data and can delete records, photos and reports from its Google Sheets and Drive at any time. To ask for something to be removed, contact the company that gave you access.
